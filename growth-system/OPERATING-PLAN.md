# Darth Algo Customer Acquisition Operating Plan

## Objective
Turn active futures traders into qualified Telegram community members, help them choose the correct Darth Algo plan, and convert them through education, proof, and responsive support.

Primary audience: retail TradingView futures traders focused on ES, NQ, MES, and MNQ.
Primary discovery channels: TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
Supporting channel: Instagram feed posts (no Reels).
Conversion hub: Darth Algo Telegram community and assistant.
Checkout: https://www.darthalgo.com/#pricing

## Positioning
Darth Algo helps active traders read direction, map risk, and follow a repeatable process without cluttering their TradingView charts.

Never promise profits, guaranteed win rates, or financial outcomes. Use chart education, transparent examples, and risk-first language.

## Core Funnel
1. Publish a short educational chart lesson.
2. CTA: join the free Telegram community for the checklist, chart breakdown, or plan finder.
3. Assistant asks:
   - What do you trade?
   - What timeframe do you use?
   - Do you scalp, swing, or do both?
   - Are you new to TradingView indicators?
4. Tag the lead by source, market, style, and intent.
5. Route:
   - New/unsure: Swing 2-day trial
   - Intraday/session trader: Scalper
   - Scalper + swing trader: Pro
   - Long-term buyer/source-code need: Lifetime
6. Send onboarding and usage education.
7. Follow up at 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days if the person has not purchased.
8. Escalate payment, access, refund, technical, or unusual questions that the assistant cannot resolve.

## Offer Ladder
| Stage | Offer | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Free chart education | Earn attention |
| Community | Free Telegram membership | Build trust and identify needs |
| Trial | Swing 2-day trial | Reduce first-purchase friction |
| Core | Scalper $18.99/mo | Serve active intraday traders |
| Bundle | Pro $29/mo | Increase value for multi-style traders |
| Premium | Lifetime $134.99 | One-time complete ownership path |

## Content Mix
- 40% chart education: entries, invalidation, risk mapping, trend context
- 25% problem/solution: late entries, overtrading, chart clutter, emotional decisions
- 15% product demonstration: show how Darth Algo organizes a setup
- 10% community/member questions
- 10% founder story, brand, and offer reminders

## Weekly Cadence
- TikTok: 5 short videos
- YouTube Shorts: repurpose the same 5 vertical videos
- Instagram: 3 feed posts, maximum 5 hashtags
- Telegram: 3 education posts, 1 poll, 1 product demonstration, 1 member prompt
- Creator outreach: 10 qualified micro-creators per week

## Backend Ownership
The assistant prepares:
- Weekly competitor observations
- Scripts, captions, post text, thumbnails, and CTAs
- UTM links and campaign naming
- Telegram conversation flows
- Lead and sales summaries
- Creator outreach messages
- Test recommendations based on conversion data

Owner actions:
- Approve public promotions and paid budgets
- Record requested face/voice/chart clips
- Connect external publishing accounts when required
- Handle escalations that require the business owner

## Metrics
Track by channel and campaign:
- Views
- Profile/link clicks
- Telegram joins
- Qualified conversations
- Trial starts
- Purchases by plan
- Revenue
- Join-to-purchase rate
- Cost per qualified join (paid campaigns)
- Refund/cancellation rate

Initial success target: determine which three hooks produce the most qualified Telegram joins. Do not scale paid media until a hook and landing path demonstrate repeatable conversions.
