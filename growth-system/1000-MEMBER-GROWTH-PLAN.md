# Darth Algo — 1,000 Qualified Member Growth Plan

## North Star
Reach 1,000 real community members who have an identifiable interest in futures trading, TradingView, technical analysis, or trading education.

Do not purchase members, followers, comments, votes, or engagement.

## Channel Targets
| Channel | Member target | Primary mechanism |
|---|---:|---|
| TikTok organic and LIVE | 350 | Short chart lessons, LIVE CTA, comment-driven topics |
| YouTube Shorts and search | 180 | Repurposed shorts plus searchable tutorials |
| Creator/affiliate partners | 180 | Micro-creators with tracked codes and links |
| Instagram feed | 80 | Carousels, proof, plan education |
| Reddit | 80 | Owned subreddit, founder reputation, approved content, ads |
| Customer referrals | 80 | Member invite rewards and testimonials |
| Website/search/email | 50 | Community landing path and follow-up |
| Total | 1,000 | |

Targets are planning estimates, not guarantees. Reallocate effort toward channels producing qualified joins.

## Milestones
### 0–100: Foundation
- Complete Telegram onboarding and Plan Finder.
- Add source tracking to every public link.
- Publish the first 10 educational assets.
- Recruit the first 5 micro-creators.
- Establish an official, policy-compliant Reddit presence.
- Collect recurring questions and objections.

### 100–300: Prove Acquisition
- Identify the top three hooks by qualified joins.
- Run weekly TikTok LIVE sessions with a pinned community CTA.
- Publish five short videos per week.
- Add member referral prompts.
- Turn real support questions into content.

### 300–600: Partner Distribution
- Expand to 15–25 active micro-creators.
- Give each partner a unique link and promo code.
- Enforce the creator posting deadline and revoke inactive access.
- Repurpose winning videos into Shorts and Instagram carousels.
- Test small paid campaigns after budget approval.

### 600–1,000: Scale Winners
- Increase output only for proven hooks and audiences.
- Retarget engaged viewers where platform rules permit.
- Promote the strongest free resource or community event.
- Run collaboration AMAs, chart reviews, and educational LIVE sessions.
- Improve join-to-activation and activation-to-purchase rates.

## Weekly Output
- 5 TikTok videos
- 5 YouTube Shorts from the same source footage
- 3 Instagram feed posts, no more than 5 hashtags
- 1 TikTok LIVE or chart walkthrough
- 10 creator outreach messages
- 5 Reddit answer drafts, published only where permitted
- 3 Telegram education posts
- 1 Telegram poll
- 1 community event or chart-review prompt
- 1 growth scorecard

## Weekly Member Pace
A 20-week baseline requires 50 net new qualified members per week.

Suggested weekly mix:
- TikTok: 18
- YouTube: 9
- Creators: 9
- Instagram: 4
- Reddit: 4
- Referrals: 4
- Website/email: 2

If the observed conversion rate is lower, improve the content-to-community offer before increasing posting volume or ad spend.

## Acquisition Offers
1. Free futures trade-plan checklist
2. Free Telegram Plan Finder
3. Weekly ES/NQ chart breakdown
4. Two-day Swing trial
5. Community chart-review event

Every offer must provide the promised value. Do not use fake urgency or guaranteed-result claims.

## Tracking
For each member, capture:
- source
- campaign
- content or creator
- market traded
- trading style
- Plan Finder result
- trial started
- purchased plan
- support questions
- opt-out status

## Weekly Decisions
- Scale content that produces qualified joins and conversations.
- Rewrite CTAs when views are strong but joins are weak.
- Improve onboarding when joins are strong but activation is weak.
- Improve offer education when Plan Finder completions are strong but purchases are weak.
- Stop channels that create unqualified traffic, policy risk, or poor customer experience.
