# Whole-business revenue operating plan

The existing nine departments share responsibility for profitable customer outcomes. Affiliate sourcing is one channel. It does not replace direct sales, product usefulness, activation, retention, or reliable operations.

This plan adapts the repository's curated page conversion, onboarding, customer success, revenue operations, analytics, experiment and knowledge-management methods. See `skills-adoption.md` for provenance and license. Customer and account baselines remain private and must be refreshed from live evidence.

| Owner | Next useful output | Completion / outcome check |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | At most three evidence-ranked revenue priorities with explicit owners | Artifact receipt and next review; no forecast booked as earnings |
| Growth | A correction to one observed purchase step; send buyers to the matching product or `/compare` | Correct link and terms; observed positive-value purchases when attribution exists |
| Content | Original product demonstration, hook, owned chart, CTA and experiment card | Creative QA, verified publication, then comparable-age reach and available downstream outcomes |
| Support | Correct first setup and a sourced FAQ/repair handoff for repeated confusion | Verified resolution; payment alone does not prove indicator access |
| Analytics | Compact revenue and funnel scorecard with coverage | Active recurring value, cash, trials and refunds kept distinct; zero-value sessions excluded from cash sales |
| Research | One buyer-intent answer or page brief with primary evidence | Useful original content and destination; ranking and demand remain unverified until observed |
| Indicator builder | Small product-reliability or clarity improvement backed by recurring need | Static review, actual compile and replay evidence reported separately |
| Operations | Bounded checkout, fulfillment or measurement repair | Exact deployment/provider readback and rollback; no repeating uncertain writes |
| Affiliates | Relevant partner kit feeding the matching product journey | Activated referrals and refund-aware paid outcomes, not prospect count |

## Measurement discipline

Review on the existing cadence. Use a seven-day operational checkpoint, then a 30-day outcome review. Sparse data is insufficient evidence, not a winner or a failure. Do not invent statistical significance or a target uplift. Compare like platform, format, intent, age and cohort. The comparison page is deployed content, not evidence of indexing or rankings.

Sessions are not unique people. A Stripe `paid` status with zero amount is not collected cash. Trial-to-paid outcomes need matured trial cohorts and positive paid invoices after trial, deduplicated and inspected for internal/test/repeat activity. Current canceled status alone does not prove a trial never converted. A canceled subscription's stale unpaid invoice is not an eligible recovery target. Reconcile invoices, refunds, fees and currencies before claiming net revenue or profit.

Use one CTA and accurate source/campaign labels on new content. Never retroactively change approved captions, hashes or uncertain provider outcomes to add tracking. Untagged traffic remains unattributed. Preserve current products, pricing, access, two daily posting slots, all-history uniqueness, approval boundaries and zero incremental spending.

No new scheduler, paid tool, outbound sequence, pricing change or offer is introduced. Existing agents consume their role-specific assignments through `operatingPlaybook()`; scheduled agents use their current role-owned handoffs.
