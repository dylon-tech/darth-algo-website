### B. That gap may be the contract roll—not the market

- Source read: TradingView, `How can I enable backadjustment for continuous futures?`, checked September 25, 2026: https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000685266-how-can-i-enable-backadjustment-for-continuous-futures/

- Factual takeaway: back adjustment removes roll gaps by adjusting prior contracts by the price difference at the switch; availability depends on the symbol/data context.

- Difference: futures-data interpretation, not chart organization or alert behavior.
- Test variable: split-screen comparison versus a single-chart reveal.
