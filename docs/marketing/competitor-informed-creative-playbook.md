# Darth Algo — competitor-informed creative playbook

Status: research-based creative direction, not published advertising.

## Verified public positioning references
- TradingView: chart-first, research-first, explain why, clean presentation: https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000570846-how-to-build-a-following-on-tradingview/
- TrendSpider: automatic technical analysis and time saved: https://help.trendspider.com/kb/automated-technical-analysis
- eToro: free Academy and virtual practice account: https://www.etoro.com/trading/demo-account/
- Webull: accessible paper trading, real-time data messaging and seamless practice: https://www.webull.com/paper-trading

These are observed website/marketing themes, not verified ad conversion results. Do not copy competitor assets, slogans, logos or layouts.

## Positioning
Headline: Your charts. More clarity.
Supporting line: Learn for free. Explore supported markets. See structure, liquidity and potential setups through Darth Algo.
Free education and practice-first positioning. $19.99/mo is a proposed future Pro price, not current checkout. Do not imply platform features are live until verified.

## Creative system
Visuals: near-black background, restrained red accents, clean chart candles, limited overlays, mobile legibility, no gratuitous neon or futuristic graphics. Use licensed chart data or original verified screen recordings. Product walkthroughs must use working features or label prototypes clearly.
Tone: plain English, useful, calm, specific, no flashy income claims.

## First 4 creative concepts
A — Chart clarity: 9:16, 15s. Hook “Your chart doesn't need 10 indicators.” Show uncluttered chart, then Darth Algo structure and liquidity overlays, with clear prototype label if necessary. CTA “See how Darth Algo reads charts.”
B — Beginner entry: 9:16, 20s. Hook “New to trading? Start with the chart.” Candles -> sweep/reclaim -> free lesson -> paper practice. CTA “Learn the setup for free.”
C — Reversal education: 9:16, 25s. Hook “A liquidity sweep isn't automatically a reversal.” Show one verified historical reclaim and one failure, each with symbol/timeframe/date. CTA “Save this chart lesson.”
D — Product workflow: 9:16, 15s. Search supported ticker -> view chart -> analysis panel -> save setup. Use working build or visibly mark concept footage. CTA “Join early access” only if a real signup exists.

## Distribution and measurement
Run organic variations on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X/Threads. Adapt first two seconds and captions per platform. Track UTM campaign/source/content, watch time, profile-to-site CTR, email opt-ins, trial activations, purchases and refunds. Confirm actual posting and clicks, not just queued assets.
Use 70% education/useful chart content, 20% demonstrations, 10% transparent offer. Never claim profits or backtest performance without reproducible evidence.
UGC-style AI narrator may explain the product, but never impersonate a customer or invent a testimonial; label synthetic media as required.

## Production acceptance
Every chart has provenance; every claim matches shipped capabilities; no copied competitor footage without license; audio is licensed/original; 9:16 exports have safe caption margins; links and tracking verified; posts confirmed live.
