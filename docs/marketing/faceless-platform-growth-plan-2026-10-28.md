# Darth Algo — faceless marketing launch plan (2026-10-28)

Status: campaign strategy and draft assets. No posts published, spend committed, or platform launch claimed.

## Positioning
Learn trading for free. Analyze markets with Darth Algo. Upgrade when you're ready.
Platform proposal: Free Academy, basic charts, paper practice, five analysis previews per month; Pro at proposed $19.99/mo. Do not market these as live until shipped. Existing TradingView product prices/terms unchanged.

## 30-day execution
Week 1: audit live publication vs queue across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X and Threads; validate existing checkout and tracking. Produce 10 short educational scripts and 4 carousels. Publish only approved, factual content through authorized channels.
Week 2: run 2 organic posts/day with variants (one education, one product or practical chart demonstration), measure retention, profile clicks, site visits and checkout starts.
Week 3: launch free Academy waitlist only if accurate landing page and opt-in consent are live. Offer 5 free previews only after feature implementation.
Week 4: compare qualified visits, lead-to-signup, free-to-paid, CAC, churn and refunds; scale only validated creative.

## Content pillars
40% educational ICT/chart literacy (candlesticks, BOS/CHoCH, sweep/reclaim, FVG/IFVG, order blocks, risk); 25% verified historical examples with failures/counterexamples; 20% actual product walkthroughs; 15% transparent offers and customer questions.
Faceless workflow: script -> licensed historical chart capture -> animated annotations -> synthetic voice or captions -> 9:16 edit -> manual fact check -> schedule -> confirm publication -> analytics.
No fabricated testimonials, income screenshots, performance claims, impersonation, or synthetic presenter presented as a real customer. Follow platform synthetic media disclosure requirements.

## First 5 scripts
1. Hook: “This wick isn't automatically a reversal.” Explain prior high sweep, reclaim and invalidation. CTA: “Learn the pattern in Darth Algo.”
2. Hook: “What does a change of character actually mean?” Show historical structure shift and one failed example. CTA: “Save this lesson.”
3. Hook: “Fair value gaps in 20 seconds.” Explain imbalance and why price need not return. CTA: “Follow for chart lessons.”
4. Hook: “A buy arrow isn't a trading plan.” Explain confirmation, sizing and losses. CTA: “Practice before risking money.”
5. Hook: “Stop staring at 10 indicators.” Show minimal chart with structure, liquidity, one signal, and risk. CTA: “Explore Darth Algo.”

## Funnel
Organic educational content -> verified live existing product or Academy waitlist -> free learning -> product demo -> paid upgrade when available.
Track per platform with UTM parameters and distinct landing page, no claims based on views alone.
Use an explicit waitlist consent checkbox; separate marketing opt-in from account registration.

## Sample campaign economics (hypothetical)
At $19.99/mo and 85% gross margin before acquisition/support, a 4-month gross contribution is ~$68/user. This is not a CAC target without observed retention and costs. Begin with organic distribution, then a small paid creative test only after founder budget approval and compliance review.

## Release/approval gates
No paid ads without explicit budget authorization. No automated unsolicited DMs. No live product claims until functionality is verified. Review finance-related advertising requirements and broker/platform policies before paid promotions.
