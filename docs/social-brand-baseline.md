# Darth Algo visual brand baseline

Policy: DA-BRAND-20261010-v3. Owner direction: October 10, 2026.

## The brand in one paragraph

Darth Algo is a bold, dark, product-focused trading brand. Black and charcoal create depth; vivid red adds energy; silver-white, heavy condensed italic headlines create the signature voice. The full-color hooded logo anchors the identity. A genuine TradingView indicator view supplies the proof. Each post turns one recognizable trader problem into a clear feature demonstration and a useful next step. It should feel confident, precise and technically capable, while remaining easy to understand on a phone.

## Keep and refine

| Element | Baseline |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Near-black #08090D, charcoal #17191F, vivid red #FF3046, silver/white #F5F6FA. Green is reserved for an actual bullish/target meaning in the product, not a competing brand palette. |
| Headline | Extra-heavy condensed italic, open counters and sharp edges. Use real fonts; the current preview uses Barlow Condensed Black Italic. Subtle silver shading is optional. No smeared bevel, cramped tracking, overlapping letters or plain system-font headline. |
| Supporting type | A deliberately chosen semibold face such as Barlow Semibold. Short lines, generous spacing, high contrast. About 44px or larger for essential body/CTA at 1080×1350; actual 390px readability wins over a numeric rule. |
| Logo | Original full-color Darth Algo logo. Clear space, consistent prominence, no AI redraw or faux replacement. |
| Atmosphere | Precision and energy from angular geometry, controlled red edge light and dark metallic surfaces. Avoid default crystal rooms, reflective floors, floating monitors or decorative candlesticks. |
| Product | An unchanged screenshot at a useful native scale, a natural wide strip or an honest feature crop. Use crisp editorial explanations outside the original screenshot. Show what the hook promises. |
| Voice | Direct, useful and confident. Plain trader language. One clear benefit or lesson, no awkward production terms, filler, invented urgency, profit promises or fabricated results. |
| Composition | A recognizable family with different structures: asymmetric feature detail, panoramic context, close-up demonstration, progressive carousel. Vary at least three meaningful choices; don't swap the title on a fixed template. |
| CTA | One concrete next step. Promotions invite exploration of the relevant tool; education invites saving or applying the lesson. Caption link must match the intended destination and platform path. |

## Production method and Instagram labels

The default is now native graphic design using genuine owner screenshots, font layers or faithful font outlines, vector shapes and gradients. Keep an editable source plus final exported image. The two October 10 v3 previews were made this way, with no new generative raster backgrounds, charts or lettering. AI-assisted planning/code is not a basis to claim that all work was human-made. The original logo's creation history has not been independently audited.

Meta's official organic-content policy says it uses industry-shared signals or self-disclosure to label generated content. Its September 12 update distinguishes generated content from AI-edited content, moving the latter's information to the post menu. A creator cannot be promised suppression of Meta's own label or detection.

If a genuinely inaccurate label appears, inspect the reason and use the platform's correction/review options if available. No disclosure setting or existing post was modified in this work.
