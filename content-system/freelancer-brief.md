# Freelancer And AI Agent Brief

Use this brief when hiring a video editor, social media manager, designer, or AI content operator.

## Brand

Darth Algo is a premium TradingView indicator brand for futures traders. The visual style is black, neon red, deep red, white, and chart-focused. The content should feel futuristic, sharp, and professional.

Tagline:

Built for Precision. Designed for Traders.

## Goal

Create short-form content that sends qualified traders to darthalgo.com to start a trial, compare pricing, or buy access.

## Products

- Darth Algo Buy/Sell Scalper Tool: $18.99/month
- Darth Algo Buy/Sell Swing Tool: $14.99/month with a 2-day free trial
- Darth Algo Buy and Sell Pro Tool: $29/month
- Darth Algo Lifetime: $134.99 one-time

## Assets To Use

- TradingView screenshots
- Indicator dashboard screenshots
- Entry, stop loss, target zone screenshots
- Website screen recordings
- Logo animation or brand mark
- Performance stats section

## Video Requirements

- Length: 15-35 seconds
- Format: 9:16 vertical
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- Captions: large, readable, high contrast
- Style: dark, neon red, sharp motion, chart-focused
- CTA: darthalgo.com

## Useful Visual Effects

- Candlestick motion overlays
- Red scanner lines
- Glowing entry, stop, and target labels
- Zooms into buy/sell labels
- Smooth chart pans
- Quick before/after transitions
- Mobile website scroll-through

## Editing Rules

- Keep the chart readable.
- Do not cover entry, stop, target, or signal labels with captions.
- Keep text short enough for mobile.
- Use red for brand emphasis, green only for buy/target/profit visuals.
- Avoid cluttered, flashy edits that make the indicator hard to understand.

## Compliance Rules

Do not claim guaranteed profits, guaranteed win rates, risk-free trading, or financial advice.

When showing performance, backtests, or trade outcomes, include:

Darth Algo is an educational and analytical tool only. It does not provide financial advice. Trading futures, stocks, forex, and cryptocurrencies involves substantial risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

## Approval Checklist

- Hook is clear in the first 2 seconds.
- Screenshot or chart is easy to understand.
- Product name is accurate.
- CTA points to darthalgo.com.
- No guaranteed-profit language.
- Disclaimer is included where needed.
- Mobile text is readable.
- Final video matches black/neon-red Darth Algo style.
